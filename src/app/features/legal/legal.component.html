Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

1. Program Description

{{ companyName }} ("Landmark Trucking" / Taylor Access / DrayTac / T-Tac Driver) operates a transactional SMS notification program for commercial drivers who work with us. Messages are limited to load dispatch notifications, schedule changes, and operational or safety alerts. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive SMS is optional and is not required to work with Landmark Trucking, create a driver profile, use the driver app, or receive dispatch assignments by phone, email, or in-app notification.

2. Primary Opt-In Method — Via Text (First Message)

Drivers opt in by responding to our first SMS. We send an initial consent invitation to the mobile number on file. The driver may ignore the invitation and continue working with us without text messaging. They must reply with an affirmative keyword before any operational dispatch SMS messages are sent.

Opt-in keywords: START, YES, 1

Example opt-in conversation
Landmark Trucking (toll-free)

Landmark Trucking: You are invited to receive dispatch texts (loads, schedule changes, and operational alerts). Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Reply YES to confirm.

Driver

YES

Landmark Trucking (toll-free)

You are confirmed for Landmark Trucking dispatch texts. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

3. Confirmation, Help, and Opt-Out

  • After the driver replies YES / START / 1, we send the confirmation message shown above and record consent.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact {{ contactEmail }}.
  • Reply STOP to any message to cancel at any time. We send one final confirmation, then stop messaging that number.
  • Drivers may also opt out by notifying their dispatcher; consent status is updated immediately.

4. Additional Consent Records

Consent may also be recorded during written onboarding or in the driver profile. This control is optional and unchecked by default — staff must not enable it unless the driver has already given express consent (for example, replied YES to the opt-in text). Drivers can use our services with this box left unchecked.

Driver Profile — Basic Information
Left unchecked by default. Only check after the driver gives express consent. Opted-out drivers are never texted.

Each driver record stores opt-in status, the date/time consent was granted or revoked, and the consent source (first-text reply, onboarding, or profile update).

5. Enforcement

Our systems check consent before every outbound message. Messages to opted-out numbers are blocked automatically and logged. Only consented recipients receive SMS.
