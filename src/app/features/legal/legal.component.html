SMS Opt-In Policy & Consent Documentation

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

1. Program Description

{{ companyName }} operates a transactional SMS notification program for commercial drivers who work with us. Messages are limited to load dispatch notifications, schedule changes, and operational or safety alerts sent through our dispatch platforms (Taylor Access / DrayTac). We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

2. How Consent Is Collected

Drivers provide written consent during onboarding. The signed driver onboarding agreement contains the following consent language:

SMS Consent. By providing my mobile phone number, I consent to receive text messages from {{ companyName }} and its dispatch systems (DrayTac) concerning load assignments, schedule updates, and safety or system alerts. Message frequency varies based on dispatch activity. Message and data rates may apply. I may revoke this consent at any time by replying STOP to any message or by notifying my dispatcher in writing. Replying HELP provides assistance. Consent is not a condition of employment or engagement.

Consent is also recorded in the driver's profile in our driver management system. The profile editor contains the following opt-in control, shown here exactly as it appears to our staff:

Driver Profile — Basic Information
Uncheck if the driver has revoked consent (replied STOP). DrayTac will not text opted-out drivers.

3. Consent Records

Each driver record stores the current opt-in status, the date and time consent was granted or revoked, and the source of consent (onboarding agreement or profile update). This provides a complete audit trail of consent for every recipient.

4. Enforcement

Our dispatch software checks a driver's consent status before every outbound message. Messages to opted-out drivers are blocked automatically and logged. Only consented recipients receive SMS.

5. Opt-Out and Help

  • Reply STOP to any message to cancel at any time (one final confirmation message will be sent).
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at {{ contactEmail }}.
  • Drivers may also opt out by notifying their dispatcher; consent status is updated in their profile immediately.

6. Sample Messages

  • "DrayTac Dispatch: New load DT-0164 assigned to you. ABC Warehouse to Port of Tampa. Open Motive app > Menu > Dispatches to review and accept. Reply STOP to opt out."
  • "DrayTac Alert: Please call dispatch as soon as it is safe to do so. Reply STOP to opt out."
